# BHARAT-AI: Research Vision

## 1. Our Mission

To research, develop and build an Indian-centred multilingual AI ecosystem with strong global capabilities.

BHARAT-AI aims to make artificial intelligence more accessible, useful and representative of India's linguistic, cultural and regional diversity while maintaining high standards of reasoning, science, mathematics, programming and general knowledge.

## 2. Our Core Vision

We envision AI that can:

- Understand Indian languages in their native scripts.
- Understand Romanised Indian-language text.
- Handle code-switching and multilingual conversations.
- Respect regional vocabulary, dialects and linguistic differences.
- Understand relevant Indian cultural and social contexts without relying on stereotypes.
- Perform strongly in global domains such as science, mathematics, coding and reasoning.
- Communicate naturally with users across different languages.

## 3. Initial Research Areas

1. Multilingual Natural Language Processing.
2. Indian-language datasets and language representation.
3. Code-mixed and Romanised language understanding.
4. Open-weight Large Language Models.
5. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
6. Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA and QLoRA).
7. Multilingual speech recognition and speech generation.
8. Evaluation of language understanding and reasoning.

## 4. Development Philosophy

BHARAT-AI will follow these principles:

- Build incrementally and validate every stage.
- Prefer open research and reproducible experiments.
- Respect dataset licences, privacy and consent.
- Evaluate performance across languages instead of assuming success from English benchmarks.
- Work with native speakers and language communities.
- Avoid cultural stereotypes and unsupported claims.
- Prioritise useful real-world applications.

## 5. Initial Development Strategy

We will progress through four broad stages:

### Stage 1: Research and Foundation
Set up the development environment, study existing models and establish evaluation benchmarks.

### Stage 2: First AI Application
Build a multilingual application using an existing open-weight model.

### Stage 3: Model Customisation
Explore retrieval, fine-tuning and specialised multilingual datasets.

### Stage 4: Advanced Model Research
Investigate continued pretraining and, if resources and evidence justify it, foundation-model development.

## 6. Initial Language Scope

Our initial experiments will examine:

- English
- Kannada
- Hindi
- Malayalam

We will expand language coverage as our data, evaluation methods and resources mature.
