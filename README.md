# BHARAT-AI 🇮🇳

### Building an Indian-Centred Multilingual AI Ecosystem with Global Capabilities

**BHARAT-AI** is an open research and development initiative exploring how to build intelligent AI systems that understand India's linguistic, cultural and regional diversity while maintaining strong global capabilities.

## Vision

To contribute to the development of accessible, reliable and globally capable artificial intelligence that represents India's linguistic and cultural diversity.

## Mission

- Research multilingual language models.
- Explore Indian-language NLP and tokenisation.
- Experiment with open-weight foundation models.
- Develop language-specific datasets and evaluation benchmarks.
- Explore regional dialects and code-mixed conversations.
- Build accessible AI applications for real-world problems.
- Contribute to the global open AI ecosystem.

## Initial Research Focus

1. Indian-language understanding.
2. English and Indian-language code-switching.
3. Open-weight LLM experimentation.
4. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
5. Parameter-efficient fine-tuning.
6. Multilingual model evaluation.

## Development Philosophy

We believe in:

- Open research and responsible development.
- Respect for India's linguistic and cultural diversity.
- Transparent evaluation.
- Data privacy and ethical data collection.
- Global accessibility.
- Collaboration with researchers and developers.
